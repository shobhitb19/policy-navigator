# Agglomeration Economies

Productivity and wage benefits that arise from the geographic concentration of economic activity. They operate through three main channels: sharing (of infrastructure, suppliers, and specialised inputs), matching (thicker labour and product markets enabling better worker-firm and buyer-supplier matches), and learning (knowledge spillovers between co-located workers and firms).

**Not to be confused with:**

- Scale economies within a single firm (those are firm-level returns to scale, not agglomeration).
- Benefits from a single large anchor firm — agglomeration refers to benefits from the collective density of multiple economic actors.

# Livable Density

An urban density level at which the benefits of agglomeration (economic opportunity, amenity variety, lower transport emissions) are realised without the disamenities of crowding (congestion, noise, inadequate green space, poor air quality). The threshold is context-dependent and varies with the quality of infrastructure, housing design, and public space.

**Not to be confused with:**

- Any specific dwelling units per hectare threshold — livable density is relational, not a fixed number.
- High-rise development per se — density can be achieved through mid-rise or terraced forms without towers.

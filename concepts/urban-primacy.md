# Urban Primacy

The condition in which one city — typically the national capital or largest commercial centre — contains a disproportionately large share of a country's urban population, economic activity, and institutions relative to the next largest cities. A high degree of primacy can reflect either strong agglomeration forces or structural distortions (e.g., concentrated public sector investment, regulatory advantages for the primate city).

**Not to be confused with:**

- A polycentric urban system with several large cities of similar size — that describes low primacy.
- A large city that is simply the largest in a balanced urban hierarchy.

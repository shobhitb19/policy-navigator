The strategic case underpinning Auckland's 2021–2031 transport planning cycle (Future
Connect, the Regional Land Transport Plan, and the Auckland Transport Alignment Project).
The document's "Access to Employment and Social Opportunities" analysis (pp. 34–35) is the
region's clearest published quantification of how job accessibility varies by residential
location and mode: it forecasts the number of jobs accessible in 2031 within a reasonable
travel time — 30 minutes by car or 45 minutes by public transport — for suburbs at 6–10 km
and 10–15 km from the city centre.

<a id="access-to-employment"></a>
## Access to Employment and Social Opportunities (pp. 34–35)

> "Figure 24 below displays the number of jobs forecast to be accessible in a reasonable travel time across the transport network by PT or car in 2031. The pattern of access generally reflects the dispersion of employment in the city, with areas closest to the City Centre having access to the greatest number of jobs – which largely reflects the concentration of employment in the city centre itself. The level of access reduces as distance from the City Centre increases in a generally concentric pattern." (p. 34)

Job accessibility table (p. 35) — jobs accessible within 30 min by car / 45 min by PT, 2031 forecast:

| Distance band | Suburb | By car | By public transport |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6–10 km | Takapuna | 200,000–300,000 | 300,000–400,000 |
| 6–10 km | Central New Lynn | 100,000–200,000 | 100,000–200,000 |
| 6–10 km | Mount Roskill | 500,000–650,000 | 200,000–300,000 |
| 6–10 km | Kohimarama | 300,000–400,000 | under 50,000 |
| 10–15 km | Māngere | 400,000–500,000 | under 50,000 |
| 10–15 km | Titirangi | 50,000–100,000 | under 50,000 |
| 10–15 km | Botany Downs | 100,000–200,000 | under 50,000 |
| 10–15 km | Albany | 100,000–200,000 | 200,000–300,000 |

Key observations from the document (p. 35):

- The Northern Busway gives North Shore locations (Takapuna, Albany) a relative advantage in PT access over car access.
- The Inner South shows stark car/PT contrasts — car access several times higher than PT.
- Areas with limited access: West Auckland (PT access under 50,000 jobs, "particularly problematic" given proximity to major employment hubs) and south of Manukau (under 100,000 jobs accessible even by car south of Takanini, likely worsened by planned residential growth in the far south).

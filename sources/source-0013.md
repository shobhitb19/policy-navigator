## Claims extracted

- [claim-0059](../claims/claim-0059.md) NZ GDP per capita fell from ~110% of OECD average in 1970 to ~79% by 2010 — entirely driven by lagging labour productivity, not labour utilisation
- [claim-0060](../claims/claim-0060.md) NZ labour productivity growth decelerated from 2.6% p.a. (1990s) → 1.3% (2000s) → 0.5% (2008–2011); deceleration driven by MFP, not capital deepening
- [claim-0061](../claims/claim-0061.md) No industry-level convergence with Australia post-1997; NZ–Australia labour productivity gap 38% in 2009 (58% attributable to lower MFP, 39% to lower capital intensity)

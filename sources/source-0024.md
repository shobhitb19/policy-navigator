<a id="slide-3-nz-total-growth"></a>
## Slide 3: NZ total population growth 2006–2025

New Zealand's population grew by 27.3% between 2006 and 2025, from 4,184,600 to 5,324,700 — an increase of approximately 1,140,100 people.

---

<a id="slide-4-year-to-june-2025"></a>
## Slide 4: Year to June 2025 — components of change

In the year to June 2025, New Zealand's population increased by 34,700. This comprised natural increase (births minus deaths) of 21,000, and net international migration of 13,700. Of the 88 Territorial Local Authorities (TLAs): 67 experienced a natural increase and 21 a natural decrease; 66 gained from international migration and 22 lost; and 44 gained from internal migration while 44 lost population to other parts of New Zealand.

---

<a id="slide-5-internal-migration"></a>
## Slide 5: Internal migration — Auckland losing residents

In the year to June 2025, New Zealand's internal migration pattern moved people from north to south. Without international migration, Auckland would have experienced a declining population. Auckland residents leaving the region moved primarily to Waikato, Northland, Bay of Plenty, and Canterbury.

---

<a id="slide-6-city-growth"></a>
## Slide 6: City growth 2006–2025

Over 2006–2025, only three New Zealand cities grew faster than the national rate of 27.3%: Auckland, Hamilton, and Tauranga — the "golden triangle". Auckland added approximately 443,000 people over the period. Tauranga grew by approximately 50% (from 107,300 to 161,000).

---

<a id="slide-9-regional-growth"></a>
## Slide 9: Regional growth 2006–2025

Northland, Tasman, and Canterbury all grew above the national average. Otago's above-average growth was concentrated in Queenstown Lakes and Central Otago rather than distributed across the region.

---

<a id="slide-11-fastest-growing-tlas"></a>
## Slide 11: Fastest-growing TLAs

Selwyn District (south of Christchurch) grew by 151% between 2006 and 2025, making it New Zealand's fastest-growing TLA. Queenstown Lakes District grew by 123%. Of the ten highest-growing TLAs: four were in Auckland, two in Canterbury (Selwyn and Waimakariri), two in Otago (Queenstown Lakes and Central Otago), one in Waikato, and one in Bay of Plenty (Tauranga).

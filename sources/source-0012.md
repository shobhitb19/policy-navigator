Cross-country study estimating agglomeration elasticities across functional urban areas in five OECD countries. Key contribution: quantifying the productivity penalty from administrative fragmentation and the mitigating effect of metropolitan governance bodies, using a two-step approach that controls for worker sorting.

## Claims extracted

- [claim-0055](../claims/claim-0055.md) Across 5 OECD countries, doubling city population → 3.8% higher productivity (pooled); range: 1.6% (UK) to 6.3% (USA)
- [claim-0056](../claims/claim-0056.md) Doubling municipalities in a metro area → 3.2% lower productivity; without a governance body the fragmentation penalty reaches 6%; a metro governance body roughly halves it
- [claim-0057](../claims/claim-0057.md) 10pp higher graduate share in a city → 3% productivity premium via knowledge spillovers (externality, not individual human capital)
- [claim-0058](../claims/claim-0058.md) Doubling distance-weighted population within 300km → 1–2% productivity premium; port cities show 2–4% premium

# Policy Levers

Policy levers are the regulatory, fiscal, and institutional tools available to governments to shape urban form, productivity, and inclusion. Each page below covers one lever: what it is, what the evidence says, and the key claims it connects to.

| Lever | Description |
|---|---|
| [Land Use Planning](land-use-planning.md) | Zoning frameworks, strategic plans, development assessment |
| [Metropolitan Governance](metropolitan-governance.md) | Cross-boundary coordination, metropolitan authorities, fiscal capacity |
| [Transport Investment](transport-investment.md) | Infrastructure spending, modal choices, accessibility outcomes |
| [Zoning & FAR](zoning-and-far.md) | Floor area ratios, density rules, minimum lot sizes |
| [Housing Supply Reform](housing-supply-reform.md) | Active enablement of housing supply — NPS-UD, consenting reform, infrastructure funding |
| [Integrated Spatial Planning](integrated-spatial-planning.md) | Long-term cross-sector spatial strategy aligning housing, transport, infrastructure and growth |
| [Infrastructure Provision](infrastructure-provision.md) | Sequencing enabling infrastructure ahead of growth; avoiding retrofit costs and leapfrog sprawl |
| [Innovation and Technology Diffusion](innovation-and-diffusion.md) | Accelerating firm-level technology adoption; agglomeration as the primary diffusion geography |
| [Investment and FDI Attraction](investment-and-fdi.md) | Raising capital intensity and FDI inflows; technology transfer and management practice spillovers |

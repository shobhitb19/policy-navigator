# Context Tags

Allowed values for the `context_tags` field in claim cards.

These describe where the evidence was gathered, not where the claim might apply.

## Income level
- `OECD` — evidence from OECD member countries
- `developing` — evidence from non-OECD developing countries (general)
- `low_income` — World Bank low-income classification
- `lower_middle_income` — World Bank lower-middle-income classification
- `upper_middle_income` — World Bank upper-middle-income classification
- `high_income` — World Bank high-income classification

## Urbanisation stage
- `incipient_urbanisation` — countries or regions at early stages of urban transition
- `intermediate_urbanisation` — countries or regions mid-transition
- `advanced_urbanisation` — countries or regions with high and stable urbanisation rates

## Regional
- `asia_pacific`
- `latin_america`
- `sub_saharan_africa`
- `north_america`
- `europe`
- `new_zealand` — evidence specifically from New Zealand

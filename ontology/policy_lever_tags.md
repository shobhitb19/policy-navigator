# Policy Lever Tags

Allowed values for the `policy_lever_tags` field in claim cards.

- `integrated_planning` — coordinating land use, transport, and infrastructure planning across agencies
- `metropolitan_governance` — formal governance structures spanning a metropolitan area
- `land_use_planning` — regulatory frameworks governing what can be built where
- `zoning` — specific land-use zoning rules including FAR, height limits, use permissions
- `transport_investment` — public investment in roads, rail, public transit, or active modes
- `citizen_engagement` — formal mechanisms for public participation in planning
- `targeted_inclusion_programs` — programs specifically designed to extend services or opportunity to excluded groups
- `housing_supply_reform` — policy changes aimed at increasing housing supply
- `infrastructure_provision` — public provision of water, energy, or digital infrastructure
- `fiscal_decentralisation` — shifting revenue or expenditure authority to subnational governments
- `immigration_policy` — settings governing migrant selection, visas, settlement, and labour mobility
- `education_system` — structure, funding, or design of formal education and training institutions
- `skills_policy` — policies for skills recognition, development, or matching in the labour market
- `innovation_policy` — government support for innovation, including R&D funding, grants, tax credits, focused/mission-oriented innovation programmes, and innovation ecosystem investment
- `rd_investment` — public or private investment in research and development specifically

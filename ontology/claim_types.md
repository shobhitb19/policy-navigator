# Claim Types

Allowed values for the `claim_type` field in claim cards.

- `causal_association` — a claim that A causes or is caused by B, with or without a stated mechanism
- `mechanism` — describes the process by which an effect operates
- `tradeoff` — describes a situation where gaining one outcome comes at the cost of another
- `sequencing_rule` — describes an ordering or precondition ("X must precede Y")
- `design_principle` — a general principle for how something should be designed or structured
- `governance_requirement` — a claim about what governance capacity or structure is needed
- `definition` — establishes the meaning of a term as used in the literature
- `model_insight` — a result from a structural or quantitative model
- `scenario` — a conditional or projected outcome under specified assumptions
- `descriptive_statistic` — reports a measured quantity, rate, or ranking without asserting a causal or associative relationship

# Mechanism Tags

Allowed values for the `mechanism_tags` field in claim cards.

- `agglomeration` — productivity or wage effects from density and co-location
- `learning_spillovers` — knowledge transfer between workers or firms through proximity
- `matching` — improved worker-firm or buyer-supplier match quality in thick markets
- `sharing` — shared infrastructure, suppliers, or labour pools across co-located firms
- `congestion` — negative externalities from excessive density or traffic
- `coordination_failure` — suboptimal outcomes due to inability to coordinate across actors
- `path_dependence` — current outcomes constrained by historical decisions or infrastructure
- `land_market_failure` — distortions in land allocation due to zoning, speculation, or incomplete markets
- `network_effects` — value increases as more participants join a network or system
- `fiscal_incentive` — behaviour driven by tax, subsidy, or fiscal transfer structures
- `human_capital` — skills, education, or knowledge embodied in workers
- `absorptive_capacity` — the capacity of infrastructure, housing, and institutions to accommodate population or economic growth without strain
- `capital_accumulation` — growth in the physical or financial capital stock available per worker
- `sorting` — allocation of workers or firms across locations, sectors, or skill tiers based on selection or matching processes
- `labour_supply_shock` — an exogenous shift in the quantity of labour available to an economy, such as a sudden change in net migration
- `demographics` — population size, age structure, or composition effects
- `amenity_value` — the influence of climate, natural features, services, or other location-specific qualities on household or firm location choice (Roback-style spatial equilibrium)
- `structural_shift` — reallocation of employment or output across industries over time (e.g. manufacturing decline, services growth) that differentially affects places
- `knowledge_spillovers` — transfer of ideas and know-how between firms or workers beyond market transactions (broader than `learning_spillovers`, which is proximity-specific)
- `technology_diffusion` — the spread of technologies and business practices from frontier firms to other firms, domestically or internationally
- `distance_decay` — attenuation of an effect (spillovers, diffusion, interaction) with geographic distance
- `proximity` — effects that operate specifically through physical closeness enabling face-to-face interaction

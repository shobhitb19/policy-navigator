# Outcome Tags

Allowed values for the `outcome_tags` field in claim cards.

- `productivity` — total factor productivity, labour productivity, or output per worker
- `wages` — wage levels, wage growth, or wage distribution
- `employment` — employment rates, job creation, or labour force participation
- `housing_affordability` — house price-to-income ratios, rent burdens, or housing cost
- `accessibility` — physical or economic access to jobs, services, or amenities
- `emissions` — greenhouse gas or air pollutant emissions
- `resilience` — capacity to absorb, adapt to, or recover from shocks
- `trust` — social trust, institutional trust, or civic participation
- `livability` — composite quality-of-life measures
- `inequality` — income, spatial, or opportunity inequality
- `urban_form` — physical structure of the city, including density and land use patterns
- `fiscal_sustainability` — long-run fiscal position of local or national government
- `economic_growth` — aggregate output growth (GDP or GDP per capita)
- `governance` — quality, capacity, or structure of institutions and decision-making processes
- `infrastructure_investment` — levels of, or gaps in, infrastructure capital investment
- `labour_productivity` — output per hour worked or per worker
- `labour_supply` — size, growth, or composition of the available workforce
- `social_cohesion` — integration, inclusion, and acceptance of groups within a community
- `house_prices` — the level or growth rate of house prices
- `consumer_prices` — the level or growth rate of consumer prices (headline or sub-components such as tradable/non-tradable goods)
- `credit_growth` — the level or growth rate of household or private sector credit
- `quality_of_business` — a composite measure of a location's attractiveness to firms, typically derived from spatial-equilibrium wage and rent differentials
- `industry_composition` — the distribution of employment or output across industries or sectors within a place
- `innovation` — rates of innovation, patenting, or new-product development
- `exports` — the level, growth, or composition of exports (including export share of GDP and economic complexity)

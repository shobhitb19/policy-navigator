# claim-0121

New Zealand has 1,175 land-use zones spread across 67 territorial authorities, compared to 13 land-use zones in Japan, a country with more than 20 times New Zealand's population.

**Source:** [Te Waihanga (2026)](../sources/source-0025.md) — [Consenting costs and regulatory complexity](../sources/source-0025.md#consenting-costs)

> "Our regulatory system is complex. We have 1,175 land-use zones across 67 territorial authorities. Japan – which has more than 20 times the population of New Zealand – has 13." (Te Waihanga, 2026, Executive summary)

**Qualifiers:**
- Presented by the Commission as evidence of regulatory complexity contributing to high infrastructure costs, not as a controlled comparison accounting for other institutional differences between New Zealand and Japan's planning systems.
- No citation/footnote number is attached to this specific comparison in the source text, unlike many other quantitative claims in the Plan.

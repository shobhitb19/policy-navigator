# claim-0086

Auckland's productivity premium over the rest of New Zealand is only approximately 15%, substantially lower than the 30–40% premium that comparable peer capital cities such as Dublin, Helsinki, and Tel Aviv enjoy over their national averages.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0020.md) — [In focus: Auckland's productivity gap](../sources/source-0020.md#in-focus-productivity)

> "Auckland's productivity premium over the rest of New Zealand is only approximately 15% — substantially lower than the 30–40% premium that comparable peer capital cities (Dublin, Helsinki, Tel Aviv) enjoy over their national averages." (Committee for Auckland et al., 2025, State of the City 2025, In Focus: Productivity)

**Qualifiers:**
- The ~15% figure is a GDP-per-worker type measure; specific methodology is not detailed in the benchmarking report.
- Auckland's labour productivity is only 3–5% above New Zealand's secondary cities (Hamilton, Tauranga) — unusually small for a primate city.

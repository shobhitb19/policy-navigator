# claim-0089

Auckland's annual share of new business attributable to foreign direct investment is more than 80% below the OECD peer average — a severe underperformance that limits technology transfer and management practice diffusion.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0020.md) — [Innovation and knowledge](../sources/source-0020.md#innovation-and-knowledge)

> "Auckland's annual share of new business attributable to FDI is more than 80% below the OECD peer average. This is a severe underperformance and limits the technology transfer and management practice diffusion that FDI typically brings." (Committee for Auckland et al., 2025, State of the City 2025, Innovation and Knowledge)

**Qualifiers:**
- "More than 80% below" is a relative comparison to the OECD peer average; the absolute FDI level is not specified.
- FDI-driven technology transfer is one of four diffusion channels identified in Winter et al. (2025); Auckland's underperformance on this channel is consistent with NZ-wide data (claim-0084).
- Some FDI comparisons are sensitive to small-economy structural factors (e.g., the Irish FDI share is inflated by multinational booking effects).

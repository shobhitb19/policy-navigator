# claim-0093

BusinessNZ projects a New Zealand labour force shortage of 250,000 people by 2045; the dependency ratio is forecast to rise from 54% (2025) to 73% by 2073 as the workforce ages.

**Source:** [Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman (2026)](../sources/source-0021.md) — [Section 2: Declining fertility](../sources/source-0021.md#section-2-fertility)

> "BusinessNZ predicts a labour shortage of 250,000 people by 2045. Overall labour force participation rate is forecast to fall from 70.5% (2021) to 63.22% (2073)." (Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman, 2026, Section 2, citing BusinessNZ)

**Qualifiers:**
- The 250,000 figure is a projection from BusinessNZ, not an official government estimate; assumptions are not fully specified.
- Primary school rolls forecast to decrease by ~7% (≈36,700 children) from 2024 to 2034, indicating the demographic constraint is already visible in the school system.
- Immigration is the primary policy lever for workforce growth in the short to medium term (see claim-0094), but must be accompanied by capital and infrastructure investment to avoid productivity dilution.

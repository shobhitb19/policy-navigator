# claim-0117

In the year to June 2025, New Zealand's population grew by 34,700, comprising natural increase of 21,000 (births minus deaths) and net international migration of 13,700; 21 of New Zealand's 88 territorial local authorities experienced natural population decrease.

**Source:** [Kanoa RD (2026)](../sources/source-0024.md) — [Slide 4: Year to June 2025](../sources/source-0024.md#slide-4-year-to-june-2025)

> "New Zealand's population increased by 34,700, due to Natural increase of 21,000 [and] Net international migration of 13,700... 67 experienced a natural increase, with 21 experiencing a decrease." (Kanoa RD, 2026, Slide 4)

**Qualifiers:**
- The year to June 2025 was a specific year with relatively low net migration compared to the post-COVID peak (2023); not representative of the long-run average.
- Natural increase of 21,000 is consistent with the ongoing fertility decline documented in claim-0091; the crude natural increase is falling over time.
- 21 TLAs experiencing natural decrease means local births < local deaths in those areas — a structural feature of older, rural populations.

# claim-0090

Auckland's prosperity pillar score dropped from the 6th decile (2023) to the 4th decile (2024 and 2025) — the largest single-year decline of any pillar across all three editions of the State of the City report.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0020.md) — [Executive summary](../sources/source-0020.md#executive-summary)

> "Auckland's Prosperity pillar score dropped from the 6th decile (2023) to the 4th decile (2024 and 2025) — the largest decline of any pillar across all three editions of the report." (Committee for Auckland et al., 2025, State of the City 2025, Executive Summary)

**Qualifiers:**
- The Prosperity decile is derived from Resonance Consultancy's World's Best Cities rankings; it is a relative, not absolute, measure.
- The absence of recovery in 2025 (remaining in the 4th decile) indicates this is not a one-off statistical artefact.
- Identified structural causes: inefficient land use, small domestic market, low capital investment (NZ ~2.8% of GDP vs AU ~3.9%), lower export intensity (~20% vs Nordic peers ~40%), management capability deficits.

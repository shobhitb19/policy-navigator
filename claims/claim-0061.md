# claim-0061

There is no industry-level convergence between New Zealand and Australia post-1997: only 3 of 10 industries where NZ lagged Australia had narrowed the gap by 2010; the overall NZ–Australia labour productivity gap of 38% in 2009 is 58% attributable to lower MFP and 39% to lower capital intensity.

**Source:** [Conway & Meehan (2013)](../sources/source-0013.md) — [Section 6: Convergence with Australia](../sources/source-0013.md#section-6-catchup)

> "No evidence of productivity convergence with Australia at industry level post-1997; only 3 of 10 industries where NZ lagged Australia in 1997 had narrowed gaps by 2010... NZ-Australia productivity gap 38% in 2009, with 58% due to lower MFP and 39% to lower capital intensity." (Conway & Meehan, 2013, citing Mason 2013, p. 30–34)

**Qualifiers:**
- The MFP vs. capital decomposition is from Mason (2013), cited by Conway & Meehan.
- Australia is a demanding comparator given its resource boom and proximity to Asia.

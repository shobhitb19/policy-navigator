# claim-0124

In 2022, New Zealand spent almost $5,000 per person on infrastructure, expressed in 2025 NZD.

**Source:** [Te Waihanga (2026)](../sources/source-0025.md) — [Infrastructure funding breakdown by ownership](../sources/source-0025.md#infrastructure-funding-breakdown)

> "New Zealand spends more than most on infrastructure. Over the last 20 years, New Zealand spent an average of 5.8% of GDP per year on infrastructure, putting us towards the upper end of OECD countries. In 2022, we spent almost $5,000 for every person in the country (in 2025 NZD)." (Te Waihanga, 2026, Section 3.2)

**Qualifiers:**
- The per-person figure is for the single year 2022 and is not necessarily representative of the full 20-year average referenced in the same passage.
- Figure is inflation-adjusted to 2025 NZD, so it is not directly comparable to nominal 2022 spending figures reported elsewhere without adjustment.
- Presented alongside the statement that, despite this spending level, "the quality of our infrastructure lags relative to what we spend on it."

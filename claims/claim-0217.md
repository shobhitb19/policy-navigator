# claim-0217

New Zealand's ranking on the Economic Complexity Index has fallen over recent years, by more than other small advanced economies, reflecting a failure to diversify its export mix sufficiently.

**Source:** [NZPC (2021)](../sources/source-0039.md) — [Chapter 2 / Figure 2.5: Economic complexity](../sources/source-0039.md#chapter-2-economic-complexity)

> "New Zealand's economic complexity ranking has fallen over recent years. It has fallen more than other SAEs (while larger developing countries such as China, India and even Vietnam have overtaken New Zealand)." (NZPC, 2021, pp. 30–31, Figure 2.5, citing Observatory of Economic Complexity, 2020)

**Qualifiers:**
- The Economic Complexity Index is based on the 6-digit HS96 classification of goods exports for 1998–2019, so it does not capture services exports.
- Rankings are influenced by countries entering or leaving the dataset over time, but the Commission's sensitivity analysis on a constant country set shows this does not materially alter New Zealand's trend.
- Norway's ranking is also falling (oil in its export mix), and Australia's ranking is below New Zealand's (commodity dominance); Singapore's has been rising.

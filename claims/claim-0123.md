# claim-0123

An estimated breakdown of New Zealand's infrastructure investment by ownership shows the commercial/private sector providing 44% ($10bn), local government 31% ($7.1bn), and central government 25% ($5.8bn).

**Source:** [Te Waihanga (2026)](../sources/source-0025.md) — [Infrastructure funding breakdown by ownership](../sources/source-0025.md#infrastructure-funding-breakdown)

> "Figure 3: Estimated breakdown of infrastructure investment by ownership. Commercial/Private 44% $10bn; Local Government 31% $7.1bn; Central Government 25% $5.8bn." (Te Waihanga, 2026, Section 3.2, citing 'Build or maintain? New Zealand's infrastructure asset value, investment, and depreciation, 1990–2022', New Zealand Infrastructure Commission, 2024)

**Qualifiers:**
- Labelled an "estimated breakdown" by the Commission, not an audited or precisely measured figure.
- Figure is adapted from a separate 2024 Te Waihanga research report ("Build or maintain?"), not original analysis in the 2026 Plan itself.
- Percentages and dollar figures are presented as a single-year (or single-period) snapshot; the underlying time period for the dollar figures is not stated in the immediate figure caption.

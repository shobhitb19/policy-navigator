# claim-0016

Auckland (population 1.72 million in 2020) accounts for 37.9% of New Zealand's GDP and 35.5% of its workforce — a level of urban economic primacy that significantly exceeds Auckland's 33% population share.

**Source:** [Gluckman et al. (2022)](../sources/source-0005.md) — [chapter 1](../sources/source-0005.md#chapter-1)

> "Auckland contributes 37.9 per cent to New Zealand's GDP and has 35.5 per cent of the country's workforce." (Gluckman et al., 2022, Chapter 1, citing Infometrics, 2021)

**Qualifiers:**
- Source is Infometrics (2021) Auckland economic profile, reported in Koi Tū (2022).
- Auckland's population share is approximately 33%, making its GDP share (37.9%) materially higher — this gap quantifies the per-worker productivity premium.
- Statistics New Zealand (2021) projects Auckland population may reach 2 million in the early 2030s.
- The Māori economy contributed approximately $9.6 billion (9%) to Auckland's GDP in 2018, per the same chapter.

**Related:** Supports: [claim-0001](claim-0001.md), [claim-0015](claim-0015.md)

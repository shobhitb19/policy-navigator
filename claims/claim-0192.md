# claim-0192

Preliminary modelling finds that urban development generates positive regional spillovers, meaning development in one location can raise welfare or productivity in surrounding locations rather than simply displacing activity from them.

**Source:** [Donovan (2019)](../sources/source-0031.md) — [Preliminary findings from the dynamic model](../sources/source-0031.md#slide-preliminary-findings)

> "Urban development has positive regional spillovers." (Donovan, 2019, slide deck — preliminary/unpublished findings)

**Qualifiers:**
- Explicitly labelled as a preliminary finding from ongoing, unpublished research; the slide deck does not specify the spatial extent or magnitude of these spillovers.
- This implies that the case for enabling urban development (e.g. through housing supply reform) extends beyond the developing city itself to its surrounding region.

# claim-0131

In response to Recommendation 1 (needs-based capital allowances), the New Zealand Government committed that the Treasury will use the Infrastructure Commission's Forward Guidance as a regular input to medium-term fiscal projections and will use agencies' investment intentions to inform advice on future capital allowances.

**Source:** [NZ Government (2026)](../sources/source-0026.md) — [Recommendation 1: Needs-based capital allowances](../sources/source-0026.md#recommendation-1-needs-based-capital-allowances)

> "Government Response: SUPPORTED. The Government sets its fiscal strategy and capital allowances for Crown-funded investments based on advice from the Treasury to the Minister of Finance. The Treasury has worked with the Infrastructure Commission to analyse how the Forward Guidance compares with forecast and projected capital spending and will use the Forward Guidance as a regular input to the development of advice on medium-term fiscal projections. The Treasury has also used agency's investment intentions as an input to recent advice on future capital allowances, and this will inform regular advice to the Minister of Finance on medium-term fiscal projections." (Recommendation 1, p.12)

**Qualifiers:**
- The Government response notes capital allowance decisions are also informed by "a range of inputs/factors... such as debt targets, operating balance targets, and economic conditions at the time" — Forward Guidance is one input among several, not the sole determinant.
- The Treasury is required to report to Cabinet on how the Commission's Forward Guidance and agencies' infrastructure plans were considered, as part of the Budget 2027 Policy paper — implementation is scheduled rather than immediate.
- Classified as fully "Supported" (not "in-principle"), the strongest response category in this document's two-tier scheme.

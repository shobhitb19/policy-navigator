# claim-0191

Preliminary modelling finds that New Zealand housing supply is affected by adjustment frictions — "sticky quantities" — meaning the housing stock adjusts slowly to changes in demand rather than instantaneously.

**Source:** [Donovan (2019)](../sources/source-0031.md) — [Preliminary findings from the dynamic model](../sources/source-0031.md#slide-preliminary-findings)

> Housing supply is affected by adjustment frictions ("sticky quantities"). (Donovan, 2019, slide deck — preliminary/unpublished findings)

**Qualifiers:**
- Explicitly labelled as a preliminary finding from ongoing, unpublished research; no specific elasticity or adjustment-speed estimate is given in the slide deck.
- This is consistent with, and provides a possible mechanism for, the documented decline in New Zealand's housing supply elasticity over recent decades found elsewhere in this knowledge base.

# claim-0189

Preliminary findings from a dynamic extension of the Rosen-Roback model suggest climate explains approximately 25% of the variation in New Zealand location amenities, with the top of the South Island having the most highly valued climate and, among major cities, Tauranga and Auckland having the best climates.

**Source:** [Donovan (2019)](../sources/source-0031.md) — [Preliminary findings from the dynamic model](../sources/source-0031.md#slide-preliminary-findings)

> "Climate explains ~25% of the variation in amenities. Top of the South Island has the most valued climate in New Zealand. Of the major cities, Tauranga and Auckland have the best climates." (Donovan, 2019, slide deck — preliminary/unpublished findings)

**Qualifiers:**
- Explicitly labelled by the presenter as a preliminary finding from ongoing, unpublished research extending the Rosen-Roback model to a dynamic setting; it has not been through the peer-review or publication process that source-0030's results have.
- Consistent with, but more specific than, the climate effects on Quality of Life documented in claim-0179.

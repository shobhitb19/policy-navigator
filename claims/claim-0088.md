# claim-0088

Auckland's housing delivery now ranks in the top third globally for new homes completed annually per capita, exceeding the peer-city average by approximately 60%, following the 2016 Unitary Plan and subsequent intensification reforms.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0020.md) — [Place and connectivity](../sources/source-0020.md#place-and-connectivity)

> "Auckland has substantially improved housing supply performance. The city now ranks in the top third globally for new homes completed annually per capita, exceeding the peer-city average by approximately 60%. Among the ten peer cities, only Austin delivers more new homes per head of population." (Committee for Auckland et al., 2025, State of the City 2025, Place and Connectivity)

**Qualifiers:**
- Housing delivery is measured by completions per capita, not by affordability outcomes; Auckland remains severely unaffordable by international standards (improved from 11th to 16th least affordable among English-speaking cities per Demographia 2025).
- The improvement follows the 2016 Unitary Plan intensification reforms; a causal attribution to the plan is plausible but not directly tested here.
- High delivery does not resolve the stock-level deficit accumulated over decades.

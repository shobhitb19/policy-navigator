# claim-0199

Doubling urban population density is associated with approximately 17% lower unit costs of local public services, a citation-weighted average across the collected literature that the authors treat as associative rather than causal.

**Source:** [Ahlfeldt and Pietrostefani (2019)](../sources/source-0032.md) — [Table 6: Recommended elasticity estimates by category](../sources/source-0032.md#table-6-recommended-elasticities)

> "The recommended elasticity estimate for local public spending reduction is 0.17... citation-weighted mean." (Ahlfeldt and Pietrostefani, 2019, Table 6 and accompanying discussion)

**Evidence base:** [evidence-0003](../evidence/evidence-0003.md)

**Qualifiers:**
- Local public service cost savings are not one of the five categories the authors regard as causally interpretable; this is an association, not a causal estimate.
- Supersedes the public-service-cost figure previously bundled into claim-0043 (retired), which cited a different value (−14.4%) second-hand via Lall et al. (2021) — the primary source's own recommended figure is −17%, not −14.4%.

**Related:** Replaces (in part): [claim-0043](claim-0043.md) (retired).

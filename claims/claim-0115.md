# claim-0115

Over 2006–2025, only Auckland, Hamilton, and Tauranga — the "golden triangle" — grew faster than New Zealand's national rate of 27.3%; Auckland added approximately 443,000 people, and Tauranga grew by approximately 50% (from 107,300 to 161,000).

**Source:** [Kanoa RD (2026)](../sources/source-0024.md) — [Slide 6: City growth 2006–2025](../sources/source-0024.md#slide-6-city-growth)

> "Only three of our cities had growth rates higher than the NZ growth rate — the 'golden triangle' of Tauranga, Hamilton and Auckland. Auckland increased by 443,000 people — pretty much the population of Christchurch." (Kanoa RD, 2026, Slide 6)

**Qualifiers:**
- The comparison is to the national growth rate of 27.3% over 2006–2025; other cities grew in absolute terms but below this rate.
- Auckland's growth is heavily dependent on international migration; internal migration flows out of Auckland (see claim-0114).
- "Cities" here refers to the main urban areas as defined by Statistics NZ/local council boundaries, not the full functional urban area in some cases.

**Related:**
- [claim-0113](claim-0113.md) — NZ total population grew 27.3% over 2006–2025
- [claim-0114](claim-0114.md) — Auckland's population growth is driven entirely by international migration; internal migration is net negative
- [claim-0116](claim-0116.md) — Selwyn District and Queenstown Lakes among the fastest-growing TLAs; growth heavily concentrated in a small number of areas

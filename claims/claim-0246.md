# claim-0246

Well over 60% of leading New Zealand companies are headquartered in Auckland, and the share is rising as restructuring and M&A centralise corporate functions; Auckland is New Zealand's only city with an internationally competitive city centre job density.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland & TBoC (2026)](../sources/source-0048.md) — [Focus area 1: gateway assets](../sources/source-0048.md#gateway-assets)

> "Well over 60% of leading New Zealand companies are currently hosted in Auckland, with figures rising amid corporate restructuring, mergers and acquisitions (M&A) and portfolio shifts that tend to centralise functions. Concentration is a big advantage to corporates looking for a credible base. Auckland is New Zealand's only city with an internationally competitive city centre job density, ahead of several recognised European and North American city centres. Auckland's concentration creates a thicker market for specialised services, whether corporate law, finance, design, media, consulting, logistics, events or technology services." (The Business of Cities & Committee for Auckland, 2026, p. 18)

**Qualifiers:**
- Auckland measure derived from the Deloitte company database (full 332-firm list, 2025); peer figures from national top-100-by-revenue lists, which the report flags as indicative given differing definitions.
- This corroborates, on a later vintage and different data source, the 61% top-200-companies figure from MartinJenkins (claim-0236). The directional finding — that the share is *rising* — is new.
- The "internationally competitive job density" statement should be read against the measured figure: Auckland's peak CBD job density of 37,000 workers/km² sits just below the peer average, not above it (claim-0248). "Competitive" here means within the peer field, not leading it.

# claim-0081

Approximately 95% of New Zealand firms innovate by adopting or adapting technologies developed elsewhere; only approximately 5% create new-to-world products or processes — making diffusion, not invention, the dominant innovation pathway for the economy.

**Source:** [Winter et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0019.md) — [Section 3: Capital intensity and innovation in NZ](../sources/source-0019.md#section-3-capital-innovation-nz)

> "Approximately 95% of New Zealand firms innovate by adopting or adapting technologies developed elsewhere; only approximately 5% create new-to-world products or processes." (Winter et al., 2025, AN 25/12, Section 3, citing Business Operations Survey 2023)

**Qualifiers:**
- Source is Business Operations Survey 2023; the 95/5 split is approximate.
- The implication is that improving the speed and breadth of technology adoption matters more than frontier R&D subsidies alone.
- This does not mean R&D is unimportant — frontier firms that create also diffuse.

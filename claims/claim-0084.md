# claim-0084

New Zealand sits below the OECD median on three of four key technology diffusion channels: import intensity of manufactures, foreign direct investment stock, and infrastructure quality — limiting the pathways through which frontier technology reaches domestic firms.

**Source:** [Winter et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0019.md) — [Section 4: Technology diffusion channels](../sources/source-0019.md#section-4-diffusion-channels)

> "NZ is below OECD median on import intensity of manufactures and intermediate goods... NZ FDI stock is below OECD median... NZ is below OECD median on rail and road quality metrics." (Winter et al., 2025, AN 25/12, Section 4)

**Qualifiers:**
- NZ is above OECD median on imports of services (a partial offset to the manufactures deficit) and on urban population share.
- The Australian Productivity Commission (2023) estimated that reviving technology diffusion could lift Australian GDP by 1.6–4.9%.
- Infrastructure deficits are relevant to agglomeration: Winter et al. cite Haskel & Westlake (2022) on dynamic cities requiring effective transport and density.

# claim-0074

Wellington's labour productivity is 4.2% below Auckland on raw measures but Wellington's MFP is 3.8% above Auckland (unadjusted) and 2.7% above (quality-adjusted), reflecting its concentration in government and high-value professional services.

**Source:** [Maré (2016)](../sources/source-0017.md) — [Section 4.1: Labour productivity and MFP premiums](../sources/source-0017.md#section-4-1-productivity-premium)

> Wellington labour productivity 4.2% below Auckland; Wellington MFP (unadjusted) 3.8% above Auckland; Wellington MFP (quality-adjusted) 2.7% above Auckland. (Maré, 2016, p. 15)

**Qualifiers:**
- The divergence between labour productivity (Wellington lower) and MFP (Wellington higher) is consistent with Wellington having high value-added per hour worked in public administration, finance, and professional services.
- This finding does not mean Wellington is a more productive urban environment than Auckland in an agglomeration sense: MFP here reflects sectoral composition, not city-level scale economies.
- Finance and insurance has the largest Auckland premium (41% above other urban areas — see Section 4.3), suggesting Auckland dominates in traded financial services despite Wellington's government concentration.

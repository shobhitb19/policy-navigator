# claim-0097

Auckland's ethnic minority communities contributed approximately NZ$50 billion to Auckland's GDP in 2023; cultural diversity is positively associated with innovation at the regional level, including through cross-fertilisation of ideas and increased aggregate demand.

**Source:** [Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman (2026)](../sources/source-0021.md) — [Section 5: Growing ethnic and cultural diversity](../sources/source-0021.md#section-5-diversity)

> "Ethnic minority communities contributed approximately $50 billion to Auckland's GDP in 2023 (Eaqub & Sohanpal, 2026). A study of 12 European countries found that 'innovation levels are positively associated with cultural diversity of the migrant community' (Ozgen, Nijkamp, & Poot, 2011)." (Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman, 2026, Section 5)

**Qualifiers:**
- The NZ$50 billion figure is from Eaqub & Sohanpal (2026), cited in Lala et al.; it is not directly replicated in this source.
- The Ozgen et al. (2011) study covers 12 European countries; its transferability to Auckland/NZ context is plausible but not directly tested.
- 16% of New Zealanders reported experiencing racial discrimination in their lifetime (Ministry of Health, 2025 NZ Health Survey) — indicating that diversity benefits are not automatic and require active inclusion.

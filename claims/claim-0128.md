# claim-0128

The New Zealand Government "Supported" or "Supported In-Principle" all 16 recommendations put forward in the National Infrastructure Plan 2026.

**Source:** [NZ Government (2026)](../sources/source-0026.md) — [Ministerial Foreword](../sources/source-0026.md#ministerial-foreword)

> "The Government has 'Supported' or 'Supported In-Principle' all 16 recommendations put forward in the Plan. It is also encouraging that the Commission's Top 10 Priorities for the Decade Ahead reflects work already underway by the Government." (Ministerial Foreword, p.2)

**Qualifiers:**
- "Supported" and "Supported in-principle" are formally defined categories, not unconditional endorsement: "Supported" means the recommendation is, or will be, addressed via current or future policies/reforms; "Supported in-principle" means the Government agrees with the intended outcome but has chosen a different implementation method than the Plan recommended.
- The Government framed acceptance of all 16 recommendations as distinct from committing to the Plan's specific implementation mechanisms in every case (e.g. Recommendation 5 on multi-year budgeting and Recommendation 15 on workforce development were "supported in-principle" with alternative approaches).
- The Green Party Response separately states it supports "the 16 recommendations of the National Infrastructure Plan (NIP) in full," while flagging specific implementation concerns (e.g. GPS on Land Transport funding allocations).

# claim-0113

New Zealand's population grew by 27.3% between 2006 and 2025, from 4,184,600 to 5,324,700 — an increase of approximately 1.14 million people over two decades.

**Source:** [Kanoa RD (2026)](../sources/source-0024.md) — [Slide 3: NZ total population growth](../sources/source-0024.md#slide-3-nz-total-growth)

> "Our population was 4,184,600 [in 2006]... Our population was 5,324,700 [in 2025]... New Zealand's population grew by 27.3%." (Kanoa RD, 2026, Slide 3)

**Qualifiers:**
- Population figures are Statistics NZ estimates; the 2025 figure is a subnational population estimate as at 30 June 2025.
- Growth was unevenly distributed across regions and cities; aggregate national growth masks significant sub-national divergence (see claim-0115, claim-0116).

**Related:**
- [claim-0091](claim-0091.md) — NZ total fertility rate reached 1.55 in 2025, well below the 2.1 replacement rate
- [claim-0092](claim-0092.md) — annual population growth slowed from 1.7% to 0.7% in one year
- [claim-0115](claim-0115.md) — golden triangle cities grew faster than the national rate; Auckland, Hamilton, Tauranga only cities above the national average

# claim-0168

The structural shift from manufacturing to services in the New Zealand economy between 1976 and 2013 disproportionately favoured large cities: two thirds of the national increase in finance-sector employment over this period occurred in Auckland, even though Auckland held just over a third of the financial sector workforce at the start of the period.

**Source:** [Coleman, Maré & Zheng (2019)](../sources/source-0029.md) — [Overview: sectoral shift favouring large cities — finance employment example](../sources/source-0029.md#overview-finance-shift)

> "The changing nature of jobs has disproportionately favoured large 'super cities' ... two thirds of the national increase in employment in the finance sector between 1976 and 2013 took place in Auckland, even though Auckland started with just over a third of the financial sector workforce at the start of this period." (Coleman, Maré & Zheng, 2019, Overview)

**Qualifiers:**
- This is a single-sector example illustrating a broader pattern; the wholesale-sector example in claim-0169 shows a similar but smaller disproportionate gain for Auckland.

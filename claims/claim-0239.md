# claim-0239

Auckland's population grew about 1.8% a year over the two decades to 2021 — against 1.4% nationally — and almost 40% of Aucklanders were born overseas, roughly double the share in the rest of New Zealand.

**Source:** [MartinJenkins (2022)](../sources/source-0046.md) — [Population](../sources/source-0046.md#demographics)

> "Auckland's population has grown about 1.8% a year over the previous 20 years, higher than the national rate of 1.4% a year. … Almost 40% of Aucklanders were born overseas, compared to 20% for the rest of New Zealand." (Chen, Knuckey & Jones, 2022, p. 5)

**Qualifiers:**
- Auckland's population fell for the first time in 2021, attributed to COVID-19 border restrictions — a temporary interruption of the long-run trend.
- The overseas-born share is the concrete demographic expression of migration-led metropolitanisation: international migration lands disproportionately in Auckland (see claims 0114–0115, 0155).
- Estimated resident population data at 2021 boundaries.

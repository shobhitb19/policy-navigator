# claim-0017

Auckland road congestion imposes an estimated economic cost of approximately NZ$1.3 billion per year, making it a significant drag on regional productivity and a contributing factor to Auckland's relatively low quality-of-life rankings.

**Source:** [Gluckman et al. (2022)](../sources/source-0005.md) — [chapter 2 (scenario 6)](../sources/source-0005.md#chapter-2-transport)

> "The economic cost of congestion in Auckland is estimated at about $1.3 billion per year (Leung et al., 2017)." (Gluckman et al., 2022, Chapter 2, Scenario 6)

**Qualifiers:**
- Source is Leung et al. (2017), an NZIER study for the Employers and Manufacturers Association; Koi Tū (2022) cites this figure.
- The $1.3 billion estimate is in NZD and reflects conditions c.2017; congestion has likely worsened since.
- This is a single cost-model estimate; methodology details, scope assumptions, and uncertainty bounds are in the primary Leung et al. study.
- Transportation tops the list of quality-of-life concerns in Auckland resident surveys (Allpress & Reid, 2020, cited in Koi Tū 2022).

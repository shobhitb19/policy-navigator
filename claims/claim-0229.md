# claim-0229

Forecast job accessibility in Auckland varies starkly by residential location and mode: within a reasonable travel time (30 minutes by car; 45 minutes by public transport, 2031 forecast), a resident of Mount Roskill can reach 500,000–650,000 jobs by car, while fewer than 50,000 jobs are reachable by public transport from Māngere, Titirangi, Botany Downs or Kohimarama, and fewer than 100,000 jobs are reachable even by car south of Takanini.

**Source:** [Auckland Transport (2021)](../sources/source-0043.md) — [Access to Employment (pp. 34–35)](../sources/source-0043.md#access-to-employment)

> "Jobs accessible from Mount Roskill: by car: 500,000 to 650,000 … Jobs accessible from Mangere: by car: 400,000 to 500,000; by PT: under 50,000 … access by both PT and car south of Manukau is low, with under 100,000 jobs accessible even by car south of Takanini." (Auckland Transport, 2021, p. 35)

**Qualifiers:**
- Figures are 2031 forecasts of jobs accessible across the transport network, not current-year measurements; "reasonable travel time" is defined as 30 minutes by car or 45 minutes by public transport.
- The pattern partly reflects employment geography (the large inner-south employment areas around Penrose, East Tāmaki, Manukau and the airport), not only network performance — some areas of comparable distance to the centre have very different access because employment is unevenly distributed.
- West Auckland's PT access (under 50,000 jobs) is described as "particularly problematic" given its relative proximity to major employment hubs; access south of Takanini is likely to be further affected by planned residential growth in the far south.

# claim-0134

In response to Recommendation 14 (accelerated electricity investment), the New Zealand Government's Gas Transition Loan Guarantee Scheme, announced as part of Budget 2026, is expected to make up to $1.2 billion of bank loans available to businesses looking to reduce their reliance on gas.

**Source:** [NZ Government (2026)](../sources/source-0026.md) — [Recommendation 14: Accelerated electricity investment](../sources/source-0026.md#recommendation-14-accelerated-electricity-investment)

> "The Gas Transition Loan Guarantee Scheme announced as part of Budget 2026, which is expected to make up to $1.2 billion of bank loans available to businesses looking to reduce their reliance on gas." (Recommendation 14, p.29)

**Qualifiers:**
- This measure is one of five listed under the Government's overall "Supported" response to Recommendation 14, alongside Fast-track Approvals Act 2024 consenting changes, a proposed winter energy reliability obligation (public consultation to 21 July 2026), stronger Electricity Authority enforcement powers, and enhanced gas supply/demand information disclosure under the Gas Act 1992.
- The scheme is administered by the Treasury and scheduled to launch in July or August 2026, available for three years — a forward commitment, not yet in effect at time of publication.
- The Green Party Response explicitly dissents from a related element of the Government's electricity/energy work programme, stating it "strongly disagree[s] with the Government's commitment to an LNG terminal" and instead calls for "a National Electrification Strategy to secure a zero carbon and affordable energy future."

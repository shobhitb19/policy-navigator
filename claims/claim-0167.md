# claim-0167

Manufacturing's share of national employment in New Zealand more than halved between 1976 and 2013, falling from 25% of jobs to less than 10%.

**Source:** [Coleman, Maré & Zheng (2019)](../sources/source-0029.md) — [Section 1.1: National decline of manufacturing employment 1976-2013](../sources/source-0029.md#section-1-1-manufacturing-decline)

> "In 1976, manufacturing accounted for 25% of jobs in New Zealand ... By 2013 manufacturing accounted for less than 10% of national employment." (Coleman, Maré & Zheng, 2019, Section 1.1)

**Qualifiers:**
- This is an economy-wide statistic; the geographic distribution of the decline varied substantially by city and by whether the manufacturing was regionally specialised or widely distributed (see claim-0171).

- [claim-0168](claim-0168.md) — Services sector growth disproportionately favoured large cities

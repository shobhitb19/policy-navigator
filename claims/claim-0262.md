# claim-0262

Auckland's innovation ecosystem rose 11 places to 75th strongest globally, its enterprise value multiplied 19 times since 2019, and it is the only OECD city among the top 17 "rising stars" of more than 300 assessed ecosystems.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland & TBoC (2026)](../sources/source-0048.md) — [Focus area 2: innovation and talent engine](../sources/source-0048.md#innovation-talent)

> "Auckland now stands out as one of the innovation improvers of this cycle. It is up to 17th 'rising star' out of more than 300 cities, the only OECD city to achieve this feat and one of only seven outside Asia. … Auckland's enterprise value - that is the value of more innovative businesses that have received investment to grow - has multiplied 19 times since 2019." (p. 26)
>
> "This rise means Auckland is overall up 11 places worldwide to 75th strongest business ecosystem, ahead of peers, Portland and Fukuoka, and the likes of Lisbon (87th) and Bogotá (125th). It is decisively clear of Wellington (141st) and Christchurch (261st), especially at the point companies reach breakout stage. Recent pace of total funding growth (+117%) is among the fastest in its cohort, second only to Austin." (p. 26)

**Qualifiers:**
- Dealroom.co Global Tech Ecosystem Index 2026 and Startup Genome Global Startup Ecosystem Report 2026. Enterprise-value multiples measured from a 2019 base are highly sensitive to that base; a 19× rise from a small starting stock is a smaller absolute gain than the multiple suggests.
- The margin over Wellington (141st) and Christchurch (261st) is the spatially significant part: New Zealand's innovation economy is not merely Auckland-weighted but Auckland-dominated, and the gap widens at breakout stage. This is consistent with the frontier-firm concentration finding (claim-0233) on a different and more recent data source.
- Sector niches cited: 72nd for AI, 74th for fintech, top 40 for foodtech in absolute scale, top 25 for space tech (up 51 places in a year), agritech up 67 places.
- The 2030 "signal to watch" is a rise into the global top 60 startup ecosystems.

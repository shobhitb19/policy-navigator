# claim-0114

In the year to June 2025, Auckland's net internal migration was negative — without international migration, Auckland's population would have declined; internal out-migrants moved primarily to Waikato, Northland, Bay of Plenty, and Canterbury.

**Source:** [Kanoa RD (2026)](../sources/source-0024.md) — [Slide 5: Internal migration](../sources/source-0024.md#slide-5-internal-migration)

> "Without international migration, Auckland would have a declining population. Auckland Council's analysis has looked at where Aucklanders go to — the top four regions are Waikato, Northland, Bay of Plenty and Canterbury." (Kanoa RD, 2026, Slide 5)

**Qualifiers:**
- This is for the year to June 2025 only; the pattern is consistent with longer-run trends but is not a 20-year average.
- The claim is about net internal migration, not gross flows; some people also move into Auckland from other parts of NZ.
- The primary driver cited in the presentation for internal out-migration is job opportunities and housing affordability.

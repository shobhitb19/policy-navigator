# claim-0169

Based on its 1976 share of national wholesale-trade employment, Auckland would have been expected to capture 56% of the national increase in wholesale-sector jobs between 1976 and 2013; it actually captured 79% — wholesale employment grew 42% faster in Auckland than its initial industry share would predict.

**Source:** [Coleman, Maré & Zheng (2019)](../sources/source-0029.md) — [Section 2.1: Auckland wholesale sector — actual vs expected growth](../sources/source-0029.md#section-2-1-wholesale-example)

> "Based on its share of national wholesale trade employment in 1976, Auckland could have been expected to get 56% of the national increase in wholesale sector employment between 1976 and 2013. In reality, Auckland gained 79% of the national increase ... wholesale sector employment increased 42% faster in Auckland than could have been expected." (Coleman, Maré & Zheng, 2019, Section 2.1)

**Qualifiers:**
- This is a location-quotient-based expected-share calculation for a single sector; it illustrates the same agglomeration tailwind documented economy-wide in claim-0168.

# claim-0082

New Zealand's gross domestic expenditure on R&D is approximately 1.5% of GDP, against an OECD average of approximately 2.7%; business R&D is roughly 1% of GDP; and New Zealand conducts only 0.02% of total OECD R&D.

**Source:** [Winter et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0019.md) — [Section 3: Capital intensity and innovation in NZ](../sources/source-0019.md#section-3-capital-innovation-nz)

> "Gross domestic expenditure on R&D (GERD) was NZ$6.4 billion in 2024, approximately 1.5% of GDP, against an OECD average of approximately 2.7%; business R&D (BERD) is roughly 1% of GDP. New Zealand conducts only 0.02% of total OECD R&D." (Winter et al., 2025, AN 25/12, Section 3)

**Qualifiers:**
- GERD figure is in current NZD for 2024; OECD average is for a comparable recent year.
- The 0.02% OECD R&D share reflects both NZ's small economy size and below-average R&D intensity.
- Given that 95% of NZ firms are adopters rather than creators (claim-0081), the primary lever is diffusion rather than R&D spending alone.

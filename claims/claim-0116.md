# claim-0116

Selwyn District (south of Christchurch) grew by 151% between 2006 and 2025 — New Zealand's fastest-growing territorial authority; Queenstown Lakes grew 123%; of the ten fastest-growing TLAs, four were in Auckland, two in Canterbury, two in Otago, and one each in Waikato and Bay of Plenty.

**Source:** [Kanoa RD (2026)](../sources/source-0024.md) — [Slide 11: Fastest-growing TLAs](../sources/source-0024.md#slide-11-fastest-growing-tlas)

> "Selwyn District, just south of Christchurch is New Zealand's fastest-growing territorial authority. From 2006 to 2025 it increased by 151%. Queenstown Lakes District increased by 123%. Of the ten highest growing Territorial Local Authorities over that period: Four were in Auckland..." (Kanoa RD, 2026, Slide 11)

**Qualifiers:**
- Selwyn's growth reflects post-Canterbury earthquake (2010–11) population redistribution from Christchurch as well as greenfield development.
- Queenstown Lakes' growth reflects tourism and amenity-driven migration, which has different economic characteristics from agglomeration-driven urban growth.
- Growth concentration in a small number of TLAs implies that the majority of NZ's 88 TLAs grew slowly or declined.

# claim-0096

Auckland will become a majority non-Pākehā city before the early 2040s: by 2043, the city is projected to be 42% Asian, 45% NZ European, 18% Pacific, and 13% Māori — a transformation that is faster and more pronounced than national trends.

**Source:** [Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman (2026)](../sources/source-0021.md) — [Section 5: Growing ethnic and cultural diversity](../sources/source-0021.md#section-5-diversity)

> "By 2043: 42% of Auckland will identify as Asian (vs 31% NZ-wide); 45% NZ European Pākehā (minority in the city, vs 55% nationally); 18% Pacific; 13% Māori. Auckland will be a majority non-Pākehā city before the early 2040s." (Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman, 2026, Section 5)

**Qualifiers:**
- Projections are based on Stats NZ demographic projections; multi-ethnic identification is increasingly common and may understate or alter how these categories evolve.
- National trend: NZ European Pākehā 65% (2025) → 55% (2043); Asian 21% → 31%.
- Auckland's ethnic minority communities contributed approximately $50 billion to Auckland's GDP in 2023 (Eaqub & Sohanpal, 2026) — see claim-0097.
- These changes will require adaptation in service delivery, housing, and planning systems not primarily designed for the emerging demographic majority.

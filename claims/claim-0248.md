# claim-0248

Auckland's peak city centre job density is about 37,000 workers per square kilometre — just below the peer-city average, ahead of Washington, Melbourne, Perth, Vancouver, Calgary and Adelaide, but far behind Boston at about 96,000.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland & TBoC (2026)](../sources/source-0048.md) — [Focus area 1: gateway assets](../sources/source-0048.md#gateway-assets)

> "Auckland's peak job density in context. Workers per square kilometre, central business district." Auckland: 37,000. Boston: 96,000. (The Business of Cities & Committee for Auckland, 2026, p. 20, chart; TBoC research based on Infometrics and Stats NZ)

**Qualifiers:**
- The chart plots fourteen centres ordered by density. Reading left to right: Boston, Chicago, Brisbane, Barcelona, *peer average*, **Auckland**, Washington, Melbourne, Perth, Vancouver, Calgary, Adelaide, Wellington, Christchurch. Only Boston and Auckland carry printed values, so the intermediate figures are not recoverable from the published chart.
- Auckland therefore sits immediately *below* the peer average, not above it — a material qualification to the accompanying text's description of Auckland as having "an internationally competitive city centre job density" (claim-0246).
- CBD boundary definitions vary between jurisdictions; the report treats peer figures as indicative.
- This is peak density within the CBD, not average density across the centre, and says nothing about density across the wider metropolitan area, where Auckland sits in the bottom decile globally (claim-0259).

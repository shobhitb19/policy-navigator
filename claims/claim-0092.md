# claim-0092

New Zealand's annual population growth slowed from 1.7% (June 2023–June 2024) to 0.7% (June 2024–June 2025); the population is forecast to grow from approximately 5.34 million (2025) to 6.53 million by 2048 on the Stats NZ 50th-percentile projection.

**Source:** [Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman (2026)](../sources/source-0021.md) — [Section 1: Slowing population growth](../sources/source-0021.md#section-1-population-growth)

> "Annual population growth has already slowed sharply: from 1.7% (June 2023–June 2024) to 0.7% (June 2024–June 2025)... New Zealand's population is forecast to grow from approximately 5.34 million in 2025 to 6.53 million by 2048, based on Stats NZ 50th-percentile projections." (Lala, Spoonley & Gluckman, 2026, Section 1, citing Stats NZ 2025)

**Qualifiers:**
- The 50th-percentile projection implies substantial uncertainty; higher and lower scenarios exist.
- Growth slowdown is driven by a combination of falling fertility and net emigration of New Zealand citizens.
- Regional divergence: metro areas growing primarily via international immigration; rural areas declining since 2019.

# claim-0080

New Zealand's share of firms using fully up-to-date technology declined from 51% (2007) to 44% (2022), indicating that the technology diffusion problem is worsening rather than improving.

**Source:** [Winter et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0019.md) — [Section 3: Capital intensity and innovation in NZ](../sources/source-0019.md#section-3-capital-innovation-nz)

> "The share of New Zealand firms reporting their core technology as 'fully up to date': 2007: 51%; 2012: 47%; 2017: 47%; 2022: 44%." (Winter et al., 2025, AN 25/12, Section 3)

**Qualifiers:**
- Measured via Business Operations Survey; methodology and response rate not specified in the Analytical Note.
- The declining trend is from a majority to a minority of firms — consistent with widening technology currency gap.
- Capital investment is a primary vehicle for embedding newer technology vintages (see claim-0083).

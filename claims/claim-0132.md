# claim-0132

The New Zealand Government's response to Recommendation 12 (integrated spatial planning) commits to regional spatial planning, led and informed by central government, that is intended to align infrastructure investment decisions under the Land Transport Management Act 2003 and the Local Government Act 2002 within regional spatial plans.

**Source:** [NZ Government (2026)](../sources/source-0026.md) — [Recommendation 12: Integrated spatial planning](../sources/source-0026.md#recommendation-12-integrated-spatial-planning)

> "A core feature of the new planning system is regional spatial planning. Done right, spatial planning can unlock, co-ordinate and improve infrastructure delivery, housing and business development, as well as environmental protection and enhancement. Spatial planning will be carried out regionally with central government leadership and involvement. Spatial plans will both inform and be informed by national infrastructure priorities and should be guided by an understanding of the costs of delivery and demand pressures. Funding and investment decisions under the Land Transport Management Act 2003 and the Local Government Act 2002 will also be aligned in regional spatial plans, which will help coordinate across different legislation." (Recommendation 12, p.24-25)

**Qualifiers:**
- This commitment is bundled with Recommendation 11 (stable resource management framework) and is contingent on passage of the Natural Environment Bill and Planning Bill, which were before the Environment Select Committee at time of writing and due to report back to the House of Representatives by 26 June 2026.
- Under the Bills as introduced, the first core suite of national instruments (including National Policy Direction) would not be issued until approximately 9 months after Royal Assent (estimated Q2/Q3 2027) — the spatial planning mechanism described is prospective, not yet operative.
- The Government separately introduced a new National Policy Statement for Infrastructure and amended the National Policy Statements for Renewable Electricity Generation and Electricity Networks, framed as designed to integrate with the new spatial planning system once it takes effect.
- Classified as "Supported" (the document's strongest response category).

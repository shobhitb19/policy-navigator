# claim-0129

New Zealand's 2026 National Infrastructure Plan is not the country's first — earlier infrastructure plans were produced in 2010, 2011, and 2015, and some recommendations in those earlier plans are identical to those put forward in the 2026 Plan, over a decade later.

**Source:** [NZ Government (2026)](../sources/source-0026.md) — [Ministerial Foreword](../sources/source-0026.md#ministerial-foreword)

> "It is worth noting that this is not New Zealand's first infrastructure plan. It isn't even our second or third. We have been here before in 2010, 2011, and again in 2015. Some recommendations in those earlier plans are identical to those put forward in the 2026 Plan – over a decade later." (Ministerial Foreword, p.1)

**Qualifiers:**
- The Minister presents this as evidence of a persistent implementation gap rather than a lack of prior diagnosis — the problems and recommended fixes have been identified repeatedly without being durably acted on.
- The Minister attributes this pattern partly to plans previously being produced by, or closely tied to, the government of the day, contrasted with the 2026 Plan's independent production by the Infrastructure Commission (Te Waihanga), separate from government.

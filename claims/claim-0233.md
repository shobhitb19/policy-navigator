# claim-0233

Most of New Zealand's national frontier firms — the most productive firms in each industry, defined as those above the weighted 90th percentile of firm-level MFP — are located in Auckland, and these firms engage with high-productivity international firms and introduce technology and knowledge that then diffuses to the rest of the economy.

**Sources:** [Zheng (2016)](../sources/source-0047.md) — [Spatial distribution of the national frontier](../sources/source-0047.md#frontier-spatial-distribution) (primary); [The Treasury (2017)](../sources/source-0045.md) — [National frontier firms](../sources/source-0045.md#national-frontier-firms) (relay)

**Reported finding** (paraphrased — the source document is not publicly available): the document reports that most of New Zealand's national frontier firms are located in Auckland, describes them as the most productive firms in each industry, notes that they frequently engage with high-productivity firms in international markets, and identifies them as the principal route by which new technology and knowledge spread through the wider economy (The Treasury, 2017, p. 40, relaying Zheng (2016), NZ Productivity Commission, Longitudinal Business Database). The equivalent findings are available publicly in Zheng (2016) — see claim-0240.

**Qualifiers:**
- The underlying analysis is Zheng (2016) for the Productivity Commission using the LBD, covering 2000–2012, now held in the repo as source-0047; the frontier is defined per industry as firms above the weighted 90th percentile of firm-level MFP. Zheng's precise statistic is three-city: 52% of frontier firms and 73% of frontier-firm employment are in Greater Auckland, Greater Christchurch and Wellington (claim-0240), with the Auckland-specific concentration shown in the Treasury document's Figure 24 and the services frontier disproportionately in the major cities.
- The source is a Treasury draft working document (marked "Not Government Policy") relaying the Zheng analysis; the spatial distribution finding is empirical, the surrounding narrative is Treasury interpretation.
- Related Zheng finding: national frontier firms grow productivity at 0.77% per year on average — almost 1.6 times the rate of firms at the local frontier.
- This is the direct evidential link between the frontier-firm literature (claims 0211–0220) and Auckland: NZPC (2021) established that the national frontier is weak; this claim establishes that the national frontier is predominantly Auckland-based.

# claim-0085

Auckland ranks 99th globally and last among its ten international peer cities on productivity, as measured by the State of the City 2025 benchmarking report.

**Source:** [Committee for Auckland et al. (2025)](../sources/source-0020.md) — [Opportunity and prosperity](../sources/source-0020.md#opportunity-and-prosperity)

> "Auckland ranks 99th globally and last among its ten peer cities on productivity, as measured by the Resonance Consultancy's World's Best Cities survey methodology." (Committee for Auckland et al., 2025, State of the City 2025, Opportunity and Prosperity chapter)

**Qualifiers:**
- Peer cities: Auckland, Austin, Brisbane, Copenhagen, Dublin, Fukuoka, Helsinki, Portland, Tel Aviv, Vancouver — selected for broadly similar size, economic profile, or strategic function.
- Measurement uses Resonance Consultancy's World's Best Cities methodology (GDP per capita and labour productivity proxies); not a direct productivity measure.
- Consistent with other NZ productivity data but the ranking is sensitive to peer selection.

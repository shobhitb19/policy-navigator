# claim-0055

Across five OECD countries, doubling city population is associated with 3.8% higher productivity (pooled estimate), with country-level elasticities ranging from 1.6% in the UK to 6.3% in the US.

**Source:** [Ahrend et al. (2015)](../sources/source-0012.md) — [Section 4: Agglomeration elasticity results](../sources/source-0012.md#section-4-agglomeration-results)

> "A city with double the population is associated with 3.8% higher productivity when pooled across countries. Individual country elasticities range from 0.016 (UK) to 0.063 (USA)." (Ahrend et al., 2015, SERC DP178, p. 14–16)

**Qualifiers:**
- Based on harmonised OECD functional urban area (FUA) definitions across Germany, Mexico, Spain, UK, and USA.
- Two-step estimation controls for worker sorting; estimates are lower than raw OLS.
- The cross-country variation reflects differences in city size distributions, industry structure, and land use regulation.

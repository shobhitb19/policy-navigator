# claim-0178

Quality of Life and Quality of Business are substantially negatively correlated across New Zealand cities (correlation coefficient −0.57): only Christchurch, Tauranga and Queenstown rank above average on both measures in every period studied, and only 18% of all settlement-year observations achieve this combination.

**Source:** [Preston, Maré, Grimes & Donovan (2018)](../sources/source-0030.md) — [Section 3 / Figure 3: Negative correlation between QL and QB across NZ cities](../sources/source-0030.md#section-3-ql-qb-correlation)

> "QL and QB for New Zealand cities are substantially negatively correlated" — the correlation coefficient is −0.57 for the whole period. "Only Christchurch, Tauranga and Queenstown achieve [above-average QL and QB] in all periods, and only 18% of settlement-year observations do so overall." (Preston, Maré, Grimes & Donovan, 2018, Section 3, Figure 3 note)

**Qualifiers:**
- This is a cross-sectional/panel correlation across the full sample of NZ settlements and census waves, not a claim about any single city's trajectory over time.
- The negative correlation reflects underlying tradeoffs documented separately in claims 0182-0183: population growth tends to lower QL while raising QB.
